    Privacy. This page keeps a small audience log on my own server, and nothing else: when a visit started, roughly how long it lasted, which sections and which things on the walls were opened, whether you arrived from LinkedIn, GitHub, a search engine or straight here, and — if you followed a link I tagged for a particular post — that tag. Clicks are grouped into one visit by a random number this page invents when it loads: it stays in the tab's memory, is never written to your device, is gone the moment you close it, and is tied to nothing. No cookie, no third party, no analytics service, no access log, so no IP address is written down anywhere and nothing kept here can identify you. Per-visit lines are deleted after 30 days, daily totals after 90. Your sound and volume choices stay in your own browser and are never sent. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, none of that happens: the visit is counted once, and no grouping, language, referrer, timing or tag is sent at all.
